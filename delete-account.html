Delete Your SeaLog Account

SeaLog provides an account deletion process for users who no longer wish to use the application.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your SeaLog account and associated records. Make sure you have exported or retained any information you want to keep before requesting deletion.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the SeaLog application.
  2. Sign in to your SeaLog account.
  3. Open your Profile or Account section.
  4. Use the available account deletion option or follow the account deletion instructions provided by SeaLog.

Data That May Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, the account and associated user data may be permanently deleted, including information such as:

  • Profile information
  • Sea service records
  • Vessel records
  • Port history
  • Account-related data

Data Retention

Data may be retained for a limited period where required for security, legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, or technical purposes. Any retained information will be handled according to the applicable privacy requirements.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account or cannot complete the deletion process through the application, please use the official SeaLog contact method provided through the application or website.
