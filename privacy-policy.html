SeaLog Privacy Policy

Your sea service. Your record.

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

SeaLog is a mobile application designed to help seafarers maintain and manage their personal sea service and port history records. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the SeaLog application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, SeaLog may store information that you enter into the application, including:

  • Personal profile information
  • Sea service and vessel information
  • Port history and voyage-related records
  • Dates, ranks and other information entered by you
  • Account information required for authentication

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you enter into SeaLog is accurate and that you have the right to provide it.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information stored in SeaLog is used to provide the application's core features, including:

  • Maintaining your sea service records
  • Maintaining your port history
  • Displaying dashboard statistics
  • Managing your profile
  • Providing account and security features

Your information is not used for purposes unrelated to the functionality of SeaLog.

3. Data Security

SeaLog is designed with security in mind. Account data is associated with the authenticated user, and access controls are used to help prevent unauthorized access to user records.

SeaLog may also provide biometric app-lock functionality where supported by your device. Biometric authentication is handled by the device's operating system and is not intended to collect or store your biometric information.

4. Data Sharing

SeaLog does not sell your personal information.

Your personal records are not publicly displayed by the SeaLog application. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate authentication, data storage, hosting, or other application infrastructure.

5. Data Retention

Your information may be retained while your SeaLog account remains active or as necessary to provide the application's services.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data using the account deletion process provided by SeaLog.

6. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your SeaLog account and associated personal data.

For instructions, please visit the Delete Account page.

7. Children's Privacy

SeaLog is intended for adults and professional seafarers. The application is not directed toward children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to SeaLog, applicable requirements, or security practices. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please use the contact method provided through the official SeaLog application or website.
